# Polyaniline-Based Organic Devices

## Project Overview

This final-year engineering project investigates the fabrication and electrical characterization of polyaniline (PANI)-based organic electronic devices.

Three device structures were studied: a Metal–Semiconductor–Metal (MSM) device, a Metal–Oxide–Semiconductor (MOS) capacitor, and a Thin-Film Transistor (TFT).

The project combines thin-film fabrication techniques with electrical measurements to investigate the relationship between device structure, fabrication processes, and electrical behavior.

## Project Objectives

- Fabricate PANI-based organic electronic device structures.
- Investigate the current–voltage (I–V) characteristics of an MSM device.
- Study the current–voltage (I–V) and capacitance–voltage (C–V) characteristics of a MOS capacitor.
- Analyze the transfer, output, and gate-leakage characteristics of a TFT.
- Understand practical challenges in organic electronic device fabrication.

## Devices Studied

### 1. Metal–Semiconductor–Metal (MSM) Device

The MSM device was characterized using I–V measurements to investigate its electrical conduction behavior.

### 2. MOS Capacitor

A PANI-based MOS capacitor incorporating an aluminium oxide (Al₂O₃) dielectric and aluminium (Al) electrode was fabricated and characterized.

C–V measurements were performed at multiple frequencies, including 1, 5, 10, 50, and 100 kHz, alongside I–V characterization.

### 3. Thin-Film Transistor (TFT)

A PANI-based TFT structure was fabricated using Al₂O₃ as the gate dielectric and aluminium contacts.

The device was evaluated using transfer, output, and gate-leakage characteristics to investigate its transistor behavior.

## Fabrication Methodology

The fabrication work involved the following processes:

1. Substrate cleaning and preparation.
2. Spin coating of the PANI layer.
3. Film drying.
4. Photolithography for pattern definition.
5. Aluminium deposition.
6. Al₂O₃ deposition for the dielectric layer.
7. Lift-off and device formation.
8. Electrical characterization of the fabricated devices.

The detailed experimental conditions and fabrication steps are documented in the project report.

## Electrical Characterization

The characterization included:

- Current–voltage (I–V) measurements.
- Capacitance–voltage (C–V) measurements at multiple frequencies.
- TFT transfer characteristics.
- TFT output characteristics.
- Gate-leakage measurements.

These measurements were used to investigate the electrical behavior of the fabricated devices.

## Challenges and Limitations

The project highlighted several practical fabrication challenges:

- Non-uniformity of PANI films.
- Environmental sensitivity to humidity and temperature.
- Photolithography alignment issues.
- Film adhesion problems and fabrication defects.
- Limitations in available fabrication and characterization facilities.

These factors can influence device reproducibility and measured electrical performance.

## Key Learning Outcomes

- Understanding the structure and operating principles of MSM devices, MOS capacitors, and TFTs.
- Exposure to thin-film deposition, spin coating, photolithography, and lift-off.
- Experience with electrical characterization and interpretation of I–V and C–V measurements.
- Understanding the influence of fabrication processes on device behavior.
- Awareness of practical challenges in organic semiconductor devices.
